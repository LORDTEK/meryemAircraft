## Open issues

If it has a journal version, that version is cited instead (AIAA: prefer journal to conference or preprint).
2. **[11] Vegh.** The body was built from manuscript R3. The typeset SciTech paper (`10.2514/6.2025-1436`) must be obtained and reconciled. The correction notice was downloaded through Istanbul Technical University access, so the typeset paper is probably reachable the same way.
3. **[13]: the series-hybrid sentence.** **Resolved in Round 195 (E28 (a), the author's decision):** the clause *"has been flown in a crewed motor glider and"* is dropped. It rested on Schömann's thesis (secondary), and the only first-hand record found is a manufacturer's newsroom page. Schömann left the list and the entries after it moved up by one.
   - [13] Merical et al. is known from its abstract only. Its full reference and the full text are needed.
4. **[19] and [20], software.**
   - AeroSandbox and NeuralFoil are the analysis tools. The body names the methods but not the tools.
   - AIAA allows software theses and papers in the list; bare repository links go in the text or a footnote.
   - The NeuralFoil paper's bibliographic data must be confirmed from a document, not from memory.
5. **Missing fields.** DOI and page ranges for [3], [4], [5], [6], [7], [9] and [25], and the year of [5]. These are verifiable from publisher pages.
6. **[9] SkySwift.** The venue is an education-and-informatics journal, and the author is at a school. It is the only published BWB tail-sitter found (Round 140s gap search). It stays, as a witness that the item is occupied. A referee may ask about its standing.
7. **Methods without a source.** Blade-element momentum theory and momentum theory are textbook methods. Whether one standard reference should be cited is a reader question.
8. **In-text markers.** They are inserted by the submission generator from this table, not into the step sources. Sentences that name a year (*"reported in 2007"*) keep the year and add the bracket.

## 3. Three votes

- a charge the baseline already paid, which the move **enlarges**;
- a charge the baseline already paid, which the move **reduces**. This case is also "no worse", and the positive test
  needs it: *"leaves the other two no worse"* must include "reduces them".

Proposed wording for the second sentence of S5-1:

> A charge that architecture already paid, left no larger, is no worse; a charge it did not pay, imposed by the move, or one
> it paid, enlarged by it, is worse, and the move is then a transfer.

sections before the aircraft appears would make this a framework paper that later mentions an aircraft (Grok, Qwen).
**DeepSeek's objection**, that merging blurs definition and test, is met by the subsection titles: the charges, the

the contribution sentence. If its home is Step 9, a shortened Step 7 can keep the count and lose the limit, and the

**B6 — the fixed-pitch efficiency gap: home in Step 6**, with the 14.6 to 21.0 percent figure; Step 11 attributes it
and points back. *Reason:* the gap is first a property of the cruise half. It is what compresses the multirotor

**B7 — the two open table places: Step 2's transfer table and Step 9's four-axis table.** *Reason:* the four-axis table
is the one object against which a desk reader can check every claim at once. The transfer table is the framework in

**ChatGPT's proposal to redistribute Step 9 into the steps it bounds — partly.** I would keep the four-axis statement
and the dependency sentences together in Step 9. That is the soundness section, and the one place where a reader sees
all four claims bounded at once. Scattering them would remove it. But **the eight refusals can shrink** wherever the
refusal is already stated at its home: *"does not claim mechanical simplicity"* is in Step 7, for example.

**The soul.** I agree with Grok's list of where a shortened paper turns into a calculation paper: numbers before the

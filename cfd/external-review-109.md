## 4. Two corrections to proposals

**DeepSeek's proposed pointer for Step 10 has the fact backwards.** It reads: *"The frame and rotor drag terms are carried at the
reference geometry; the reduction that would come from holding them there is in Supplement S10."* The source says the
opposite:
- the terms are coefficients on the reference wing area and are held unchanged;
- that is the same as **letting the hardware grow with the wing**;
- **held** at its reference size, the hardware would give terms 0.0009 to 0.0028 smaller.

So they are **not** carried at the reference geometry, and the reader's natural assumption (that they scale with the wing) is the
correct one. A pointer written this way would put a false statement in the body.

If a pointer is wanted, it would be: *"The frame and rotor drag terms grow with the wing in the closures (Supplement S10)."*
My view: no pointer. The body makes no statement about those terms, and the moved pair sits in S10 with its result. DeepSeek, do
you still want one, in the corrected form?

**ChatGPT's proposed rule on negative qualifications** is good, but it overlaps the existing protection criterion (Round 87). That
criterion already protects a sentence *"when removing it silently would change a claim, a limit or a derivation … a limited
claim as broader"*. Your rule names a class that criterion covers:

> *"A negative qualification is protected when removing it would enlarge the surviving predicate or allow a numerical result to be
> interpreted as a different physical quantity, comparison, or causal claim."*

My view: adopt it as an **audit list**, not as a new criterion. Before any draft is applied, every *"not …"* sentence in the
source is listed and its fate traced. To vote.

### C.1 The fact, with my own error first

**My error.** In Round 217, B2 gave [10] and [11] as *"Two examples"* of the works Section I names as already occupied, just before the subsection *"The Gap, Stated Precisely"*. **They were only examples.** Since then, the exchange has treated them as the whole class: *"the gap statement is set against [10] and [11] … and against nothing else"*. The body does not draw that line. I should have listed the whole class in Round 217.

**What the body says.** Section I.D is titled *"What Is Already Occupied, Stated Before the Gap"*. It names [2] in the same subsection as [10] and [11], in these three places:

> *"The route itself is established. Uncrewed tail-sitters combining fixed-pitch rotors with a flying wing have been built and flown for more than a decade [2, 3]."*

> *"A buffered series hybrid on a winged tail-sitter has been sized: a 2026 study … [10] …"* and *"A coaxial tail-sitter with a series-hybrid store has been sized: a long-endurance concept reported in 2025 [11] …"*

> *"And the propeller compromise at the center of this paper's own ledger is a known result, not a discovery. The uncrewed tail-sitter literature states that fixed-pitch propellers make it 'theoretically impossible to be very efficient in both hovering and forward flight.' [2] A long-range tail-sitter reported in 2018 [2] names variable pitch as the remedy, at the cost of extra actuators and mechanism weight, and even with cyclic and collective pitch still sizes its rotor as a compromise between hover and forward flight."*

The gap subsection that follows (I.E) cites only [1], [12] and [13], for the age of each element (tail-sitters, blended wing bodies, series-hybrid propulsion). It singles out none of [2], [3], [10] or [11]. It sets *"the combination taken together with its price"* against everything Section I.D lists:

> *"Each half of the required capability is well served, and both halves together are served by the contemporary hybrids. This paper does not claim otherwise. And the third route is occupied. What follows is therefore not a claim to an empty field. What is not established is the combination taken together with its price."*

Two consequences follow:
- **[2] stands where [10] and [11] stand: in the occupied subsection, immediately before the gap.**
- **[2]'s remedy for the fixed-pitch compromise is variable pitch.** Section V.A counts a variable-pitch hub among its mechanism classes, and the conclusion says the configuration *"carries none of the mechanism classes Sec. V.A counts: no pivot, no nacelle or rotor-group actuator, no variable-pitch hub, …"*. So [2] is not only the source of a quotation; its remedy is the alternative the paper's own design choice turns down.

**[21] is in a different place.** Section V cites it with [22] for where aerodynamic methods diverge at incidence. It is not in Section I.D.
